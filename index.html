This is the actual COSMIC authentication system. Enroll yourself in about 20 seconds, then let the scanner check that you are live, real, and you. Your data is linked only to this browser and deleted after an hour.

Requests run on a GPU on the author’s PC. When that machine is switched off, the demo is offline.

What happens on every frame

Each check can stop the login. Cheap checks run first, so spoofs are rejected before any identity matching.

  1. Stage 0

    Liveness

    ViT classifier, blink detection and frequency cues reject photos and screen replays.

  2. Stage 1

    Deepfake check

    Frequency fingerprints and texture consistency flag synthetic faces.

  3. Stage 2

    Face + voice

    ArcFace (512-d) and ECAPA-TDNN (192-d) embeddings of your face and voice.

  4. Stage 3

    Fusion

    Both scores are combined into one identity decision against your enrollment.

  5. Stage 4

    Step-up

    A borderline score triggers a short question an LLM checks before granting access.

Try to fool it

After you enroll, hold up a photo of yourself, play your face from another screen, or have a friend sit in. Watch which stage stops each attempt in the audit trail. It isn't perfect: the liveness and deepfake gates are about 86% accurate on their benchmarks, which is why the face and voice checks sit behind them.

Your data

  • Only embeddings (lists of numbers) are stored, never your photos or recordings.
  • They are tied to a random ID in this browser and never shown to other visitors.
  • They are deleted automatically after 1 hour, or immediately with “Delete my data now”.
  • If you get a step-up question, your typed answer is checked by an LLM service (Groq).
